Research

I study where agent systems quietly go wrong: when retrieval returns information that is relevant but not admissible, and when capable agents collaborate badly.

AdmitBench: relevant isn’t always admissible

Independent research, ongoing

Retrieval systems treat relevance as permission to answer. But a passage can match a query and still be inadmissible: superseded, not yet in effect, or true for a different moment than the one asked about. AdmitBench is a statement-level benchmark for whether retrieved information is admissible for a given query, paired with an admissibility-constrained retrieval framework that reduces supersession, anticipation and recency-bias errors.
